Before you commit

Real questions, direct answers.

Will you recommend a rebuild every time?

No. The smallest useful fix may be one message, one form, one mobile path, or no work yet.

Who owns the result?

The written scope names the files, access, domain, content, and handoff. There is no hidden ownership trap.

Is hosting included?

Only when the written scope says so. Hosting, licenses, third-party services, and maintenance remain visible.

What happens after the sprint?

You can stop with the completed path, commission another bounded improvement, or scope a larger refresh separately.
