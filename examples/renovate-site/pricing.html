Good questions

A few things worth knowing before we start.

Can you give an exact price from the first email?

Usually not responsibly. The planning range helps us talk realistically; the exact fee follows a quick review and a written scope.

Can a complete website launch in 48 hours?

Not as a blanket promise. Timing depends on page count, content, integrations, feedback, and approvals.

Are hosting and paid services included?

Only when listed in the written scope. Third-party fees, licenses, hosting, content production, and ongoing maintenance stay visible.

What usually changes the price?

Page count, copywriting, ecommerce, booking, migration, integrations, content volume, accessibility, and launch responsibility.
