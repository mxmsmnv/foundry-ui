Process

Clarity before production.

The 48-hour idea belongs to an early design direction after the inputs are ready. Build time depends on real scope.

From first look to release

  1. Fit checkConfirm the business, current site, decision-maker, and concrete problem.
  2. Brief and source reviewCollect the offer, audience, actions, content, and technical context.
  3. 3First directionShow hierarchy, message, and visual intent as a decision tool.
  4. 4Written scopeDefine pages, features, ownership, revisions, payment, and launch responsibilities.
  5. 5Build and reviewImplement the approved system and complete agreed review rounds.
  6. 6Verification and launchCheck pages, devices, links, forms, metadata, and handoff requirements.
