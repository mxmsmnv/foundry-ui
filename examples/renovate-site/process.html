Start with a fit check

The working path

Every stage answers a different question.

  1. Fit check

    Is this the right problem to solve together?

    We look at the current site, the business, the decision-maker, and the concrete friction.

    Input: URL + goal
  2. Brief + evidence

    What does the visitor need to understand and do?

    We collect the offer, audience, actions, source content, technical context, and constraints.

    Output: working brief
  3. First direction

    What should the experience feel and read like?

    A focused direction establishes hierarchy, message, visual intent, and the customer path.

    Output: decision-ready concept
  4. Written scope

    What exactly are we building?

    Pages, features, content ownership, revisions, payment, integrations, and launch responsibilities become explicit.

    Output: approved scope
  5. Build + review

    Does the system hold together in real use?

    We implement the approved direction and review the actual responsive experience—not isolated screenshots.

    Output: working website
  6. Verify + release

    Is the agreed website actually ready?

    Pages, devices, links, forms, metadata, content, and handoff requirements are checked before release.

    Output: verified launch

Working principles

No disappearing scope. No fake certainty.

Written

Scope, responsibilities, exclusions, and approval points are recorded.

Staged

Assessment, direction, production, and launch remain distinct decisions.

Verified

A launch is complete only when the agreed checks actually pass.

About timing

The first direction can move quickly. The complete build follows real scope.

A fast concept is a decision tool, not a promise that every website can launch in 48 hours. Page count, content, integrations, review cycles, and approvals still determine production timing.
