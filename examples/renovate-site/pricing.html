Questions

What the range does—and does not—mean.

Is the first direction free?

A limited homepage direction may be offered after a fit check and complete brief. It is a decision aid based on available public information, not a custom multi-page build or transferable production code.

Can a complete website launch in 48 hours?

Not as a blanket promise. A first direction can move quickly once inputs are ready. Production timing depends on page count, content, integrations, feedback, approvals, and the condition of the current system.

Are hosting, domains, or paid services included?

Only when listed in the written scope. Third-party fees, licenses, hosting, content production, and ongoing maintenance remain visible rather than being hidden inside a vague package.

What can change the price?

Page count, copywriting needs, ecommerce, booking or account features, data migration, custom integrations, unusual content volume, accessibility requirements, and launch responsibility can all change the final scope.
