Process

Clarity before production.

A compact, visible workflow keeps the creative direction moving while protecting scope, ownership, and launch quality.

From first look to release

Every stage answers a different question.

The 48-hour idea belongs to an early design direction after the inputs are ready. Build time depends on real scope, content, integrations, and feedback.

A

Fit check

We confirm the business, current site, decision-maker, public contact details, and the concrete problem worth solving.

B

Brief and source review

We collect the essential offer, audience, actions, brand constraints, useful content, and technical context.

C

First direction

A focused homepage direction shows hierarchy, message, and visual intent. It is a decision tool, not unpaid production code.

D

Written scope

Pages, features, content ownership, revisions, payment, support, maintenance, and launch responsibilities become explicit.

E

Build and review

The approved system is implemented responsively, content is placed, and agreed review rounds are completed.

F

Verification and launch

We check essential pages, devices, links, forms, metadata, and handoff requirements before release.

Working principles

No disappearing scope. No fake certainty.

Fast decisions come from reducing ambiguity—not from pretending every website has the same complexity.

Written

Scope, responsibilities, and approval points are recorded.

Staged

Assessment, direction, production, and launch remain distinct.

Verified

A launch is complete only after the agreed checks pass.
