Questions

What the range does—and does not—mean.

Is the first direction free?

A limited direction may be offered after a fit check and complete brief. It is a decision aid, not a custom multi-page build or transferable production code.

Can a complete website launch in 48 hours?

Not as a blanket promise. Production timing depends on page count, content, integrations, feedback, and approvals.

Are hosting and paid services included?

Only when listed in the written scope. Third-party fees, licenses, hosting, content production, and ongoing maintenance stay visible.

What can change the price?

Page count, copywriting, ecommerce, booking, migration, integrations, content volume, accessibility, and launch responsibility.
