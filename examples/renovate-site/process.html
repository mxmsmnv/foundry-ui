How we work

Clear steps. No mystery.

You should always know what we are deciding, what happens next, and what the work is expected to achieve.

Start with a conversation

The working path

Every step answers one useful question.

The process is structured enough to keep things moving and flexible enough to reflect the actual business.

  1. 01

    Talk

    What is happening now?

    We look at the current site, the business goal, the real constraints, and what customers keep asking.

    Current URL + goal
  2. 02

    Understand

    What should visitors understand and do?

    We gather the offer, audience, useful content, technical context, and the points where people hesitate.

    Working brief
  3. 03

    Shape

    What is the clearest direction?

    A focused concept establishes the message, hierarchy, tone, and customer path before production expands.

    Visible direction
  4. 04

    Agree

    What exactly are we making?

    Pages, content ownership, features, integrations, revisions, price, and launch responsibilities become explicit.

    Written scope
  5. 05

    Build

    Does it work as a real website?

    We implement and review the responsive experience in the browser—not as a stack of disconnected screenshots.

    Working website
  6. 06

    Check

    Is the agreed work actually ready?

    Pages, devices, links, forms, metadata, content, and handoff needs are checked before release.

    Verified launch

What working together feels like

Thoughtful, direct, and visible.

Plain language

Decisions are explained without making the process sound more complicated than it is.

Written boundaries

Responsibilities, exclusions, feedback points, and the agreed result stay visible.

Real checks

“Finished” means the agreed website has been reviewed in the places people will use it.
